What is included in the service?

Nexora Webworks creates and prepares the website according to the agreed scope, content and design direction. Additional features outside the selected package can be quoted separately.

Separate charges: Domain registration, paid hosting, premium third-party services, payment gateways, advanced e-commerce functionality, paid plugins/subscriptions and ongoing maintenance are not included unless specifically mentioned in the quotation.

Terms & Conditions

1. Project Scope

Only the pages, features and functionality agreed before the project begins are included. New features may require an additional charge.

2. Content

The client is responsible for providing accurate business information, images, logo, prices and other required content unless content creation is separately agreed.

3. Revisions

Reasonable design/content revisions within the agreed scope are included. Major redesigns or new sections may be charged separately.

4. Payment

Payment schedule should be agreed in writing before work starts. Any advance, milestone and final-payment terms should be clearly recorded in the project quotation.

5. Domain & Hosting

Domain and paid hosting costs are separate unless explicitly included. Renewal charges are the client's responsibility after the applicable period.

6. Delivery

Delivery timing depends on project scope and how quickly the required content, approvals and feedback are provided.

7. Third-Party Services

Google, WhatsApp, hosting providers, payment gateways and other third-party platforms operate under their own terms and availability.

8. Maintenance

Future edits, new pages, content updates, troubleshooting or ongoing maintenance are separate services unless a maintenance plan is agreed.

9. Client Approval

The client should review the final website and confirm that the supplied business information and content are correct before final launch.

10. Legal & Tax Matters

Applicable invoices, taxes, registrations and contractual arrangements should be handled according to the applicable laws and the client's/business owner's circumstances.

For clients under the age of majority: formal contracts, payment arrangements and business commitments should be handled with an appropriate parent/guardian or other legally competent adult. This document is a general business template and is not a substitute for legal or tax advice.
